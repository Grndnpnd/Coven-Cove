# Raspka Irongrinn (Granny Threadbare)

*Large fey, neutral evil*

**Armor Class** 21 (iron-thread hide)
**Hit Points** 147 (14d10 + 70)
**Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21 (+5) | 12 (+1) | 20 (+5) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Con +9, Wis +6
**Skills** Deception +6, Insight +6, Intimidation +6, Perception +6
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
**Languages** Common, Giant, Sylvan
**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP) — **Proficiency Bonus** +4

## Traits

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Raspka's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- 3/day each: *disguise self* (Small or Medium humanoid), *fog cloud*
- 1/day: *animate objects* (her doll-toys only)

***Puppet Strings.*** Her animated toys carry thread-tethers back to her claws. While any of them is within 30 feet, she can use her reaction when hit by an attack to make one of them the target instead.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** Raspka makes three attacks: one with her Bite and two with her Claws.

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (3d6 + 5) piercing damage.

***Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15). Raspka can grapple up to two creatures.

***Crushing Hug.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, one creature she is grappling. *Hit:* 36 (5d10 + 9) bludgeoning damage, and she can't make Claw attacks until the hug ends. It ends if she releases the grapple or is forced to move.

***Sew the Wound (Recharge 5–6).*** She stitches her own wounds, regaining 22 (4d8 + 4) hit points. Alternatively she stitches a creature's *shadow* to the ground (reach 10 ft.): DC 15 Strength save or restrained 1 minute (repeat at end of turn; shadowless creatures are immune, which she notes with great interest).

*Annis hag of the Iron Present; daughter of Skabatha of Thither, one of the Three of Coven Cove. While the coven circle is dead: no Shared Spellcasting, no Hag Eye, and no shipbirth ritual. Her animate objects is hers, not the circle's, and what she has been testing it on is loose in Drydock Seven.*
