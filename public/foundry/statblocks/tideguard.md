# Tideguard

*Medium humanoid (hag-crafted), lawful neutral*

**Armor Class** 14 (patchwork scale)
**Hit Points** 58 (9d8 + 18)
**Speed** 30 ft., swim 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

**Skills** Athletics +5, Insight +3, Perception +3
**Senses** passive Perception 13
**Languages** Common, Sylvan, harbor-cant
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP) — **Proficiency Bonus** +2

## Traits

***Seam-Sense.*** Within Coven Cove, the Tideguard can't be surprised, and instinctively knows which side of the seam it stands on in total darkness.

***Hag-Crafted.*** Advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened while it can see the triple-moon sigil. (While the coven circle is dead, this is *habit and faith*, not magic — the town doesn't know that, and Barra isn't saying.)

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Tideguard makes two attacks.

***Harpoon.*** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage. On a thrown hit, the Tideguard can pull a Large or smaller creature 10 feet toward it.

***Belaying Club.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

## Reactions

***Keep the Peace.*** When a creature within 5 feet makes an attack against a target other than the Tideguard, the Tideguard can impose disadvantage on the roll by interposing shield, body, or gaff.

---

*Encounter framing: Tideguard operate in fours with a sergeant (Barra: veteran). A police action, not a warband — they grapple, interpose, harpoon ankles, and drag offenders off the seam. Four of them ≈ 2,800 XP: a Medium encounter the party* cannot *win socially, even by winning.*
