# Feral Mimic-Hulk ("the Seventh Born")

*Huge monstrosity (shapechanger), unaligned*

**Armor Class** 15 (barnacled hull)
**Hit Points** 172 (15d12 + 75)
**Speed** 20 ft., swim 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24 (+7) | 8 (−1) | 20 (+5) | 5 (−3) | 12 (+1) | 6 (−2) |

**Skills** Stealth +3 (advantage in its cradle or among hulls)
**Damage Immunities** acid, psychic
**Condition Immunities** prone
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
**Languages** understands the work-songs sung to it; speaks only in borrowed voices
**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP) — **Proficiency Bonus** +4

## Traits

***Shapechanger.*** As an action it reshapes between its true form (a galleon with a spine) and a convincing derelict hull. A creature that touches it or inspects it closely can make a DC 17 Intelligence (Investigation) check to notice it breathing.

***False Appearance.*** In hull form it is indistinguishable from an ordinary (if lovely) derelict at more than arm's length.

***Adhesive Pitch.*** Its decks and maw exude pitch. A creature or object that hits it with a melee attack, or starts its turn grappled by it, is stuck: DC 17 Strength check to free. It can release anything it chooses. It does not choose often.

***Borrowed Voice.*** It perfectly mimics any voice it has heard for at least a minute. It uses this constantly, mostly kindly.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The hulk makes one Maw attack and one Oar-Sweep attack.

***Maw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 29 (4d10 + 7) piercing damage, and a Large or smaller target is grappled (escape DC 17). While grappling this way it can't use Maw on another target.

***Oar-Sweep.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 15 ft., up to three targets in a line. *Hit:* 16 (2d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage and the target is pushed 10 ft.

***Splinter Volley (Recharge 5–6).*** 20-foot cone of hull splinters: DC 18 Dexterity save, 27 (6d8) piercing damage on a failure, half on success, attended by a sound like a ship's bell.

---

*Encounter framing: alone, a Hard-ish solo for a level 9–10 party (action-economy poor); run it with the yard as terrain — swinging cradles, pitch pools (2d6 acid), the other hulks reacting to every hit. It does not want to die; it wants to be* born. *Speaking to it mid-fight (Persuasion or Performance DC 15, advantage with a work-song) can stand it down.*
