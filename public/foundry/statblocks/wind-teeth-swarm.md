# Wind-Teeth Swarm

*Medium swarm of Tiny fey grit-motes, unaligned*

**Armor Class** 12
**Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 50 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 (−4) | 15 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 2 (−4) | 10 (+0) | 2 (−4) |

**Damage Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
**Damage Vulnerabilities** cold (water and ice knock the teeth out of the wind)
**Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned
**Senses** blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 10
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)

## Traits

***Swarm.*** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and move through any opening large enough for a Tiny mote (1-inch openings); it can't regain hit points.

***Abrasion.*** A creature starting its turn in the swarm's space takes 7 (2d6) slashing damage, and nonmetallic armor worn takes a cumulative −1 AC penalty (max −3) until repaired. Unattended rope, sailcloth, and leather in the space take 7 damage per round.

***Riding the Gale.*** In a Shred Gale or Grinder Heart its speed doubles and Abrasion becomes 10 (3d6).

---

*A sand-shred hazard given teeth. Encounter framing: 1 swarm in a Shred Gale is a Medium encounter with teeth; 2 swarms in the Grinder Heart (quest 5) is the wall-of-teeth moment. Cold damage, gust of wind, and shelter are the answers; swords are a prayer.*
