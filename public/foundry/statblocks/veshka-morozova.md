# Veshka Morozova

*Medium fey, neutral evil*

**Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 136 (16d8 + 64)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +6, Cha +8
**Skills** Arcana +5, Deception +8, Perception +6, Stealth +7
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
**Languages** Auran, Common, Giant, Sylvan
**Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP) — **Proficiency Bonus** +4

## Traits

***Ice Walk.*** Veshka can move across and climb icy surfaces without an ability check, and difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost her extra movement.

***Masthead Staff (Graystaff Magic).*** Her staff is a spellcasting focus. Its bells ring when a creature within 30 feet deliberately lies (only Veshka hears them unless she wills otherwise). While she holds it, her innate spellcasting expands as below; without it she loses the 1/day spells and *ray of frost*.

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Veshka's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- At will: *ray of frost* (2d8 + 4 cold damage and speed reduced 10 ft.)
- 3/day each: *fog cloud*, *sleet storm*
- 1/day each (staff only): *ice storm*, *wall of ice*, *cone of cold*

***Despairing Feast.*** When a creature Veshka can see fails a saving throw against one of her spells or abilities, she can use her reaction to feed on the despair, gaining 10 temporary hit points.

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** Veshka makes two Graystaff attacks.

***Graystaff.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) cold damage.

***Howling Dirge (Recharge 5–6).*** Storm-keened prophecy in a 30-foot cone: DC 16 Wisdom save, 27 (5d8 + 4) psychic damage and frightened for 1 minute on a failure, half damage and no fear on success (repeat save at end of turn). A frightened creature sees its own death staged in the ice.

---

*Bheur hag of the Storm-Cast Future; daughter of Endelyn Moongrave of Yon, one of the Three of Coven Cove. While the coven circle is dead: no Shared Spellcasting, no Hag Eye, and the omens have gone quiet — she gets static, one recurring vision, and silence.*
